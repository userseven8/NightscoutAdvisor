## 2. Informational Retrospective Statistics Only — Not Medical Advice
* All outputs generated by Nightscout Advisor are **retrospective mathematical and statistical summaries** derived from historical Nightscout telemetry (`entries`, `treatments`, and `profile` records).
* Retrospective algorithms **cannot** observe or account for sensor compression lows, calibration drift, infusion-set or cannula occlusion, lipohypertrophy, unannounced meals, physical activity, illness, stress, menstrual or growth hormones, alcohol, or concurrent medications.
* Nothing displayed by this software constitutes medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or a prescription or instruction to alter insulin therapy.

## 3. Severe Risk of Insulin Therapy Adjustments
Insulin is a high-alert hormone with a narrow therapeutic window. Modifying basal rates, insulin sensitivity factors (ISF), carbohydrate ratios (CR), or glucose target ranges based on incomplete, noisy, or misread telemetry can result in **severe hypoglycemia, seizure, diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), coma, permanent injury, or death**.

**Never modify insulin pump or automated insulin delivery (AID) settings based solely on this software.** Always review historical glucose telemetry and any potential therapy adjustments with a licensed endocrinologist or qualified diabetes care professional.
